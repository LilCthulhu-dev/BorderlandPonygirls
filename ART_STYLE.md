# Art Style Guide

Visual guidelines for new event plates, combat scenes, and other illustrations in **Borderland Ponygirls**.

## Core Look

- **Notebook-sketch appearance** – loose, hand-drawn contours with varied line weight.
- Simple, comic-like sketch style with clear shapes.
- Avoid excessive shading, cross-hatching, and ultra-fine linework.
- No gradients, photorealism, or glossy digital painting.
- No written labels inside the illustrations unless we deliberately want map annotations.

## Background & Framing

- Black linework on a **transparent background**.
- Transparent background with an **irregular vignette shape** — no rectangular sky or hard frame.
- **Portrait orientation (2:3)**.

## Colour Palette

| Role        | Hex       |
|-------------|-----------|
| Main black  | `#090a14` |
| Main white  | `#ebede9` |

- Prefer pure **black & white** whenever possible.
- If additional colours are required, use the [Apollo palette](https://lospec.com/palette-list/apollo).

## Summary Checklist

- [ ] Loose hand-drawn contours, varied line weight
- [ ] Clear shapes, minimal shading
- [ ] No gradients / photorealism / glossy finish
- [ ] Transparent background + irregular vignette
- [ ] 2:3 portrait
- [ ] Linework `#090a14`, paper white `#ebede9`
- [ ] Colour only from Apollo when needed
