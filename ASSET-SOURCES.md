**Source:** ChatGPT by OpenAI

## Wonderdraft Map

`assets/img/map.png` was created by LilCthulhu using Wonderdraft and only the standard assets supplied with the software.

Wonderdraft permits creators to publish and sell their original exported maps. However, the exported map contains standard Wonderdraft assets and is therefore excluded from the repository’s CC0 dedication.

**Source:** Wonderdraft
**License:** Subject to the Wonderdraft terms; excluded from CC0

Screenshots may contain code, CC0 content, AI-generated images, and the excluded Wonderdraft map. Their individual elements remain subject to their respective licenses.
