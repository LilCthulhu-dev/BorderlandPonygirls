**Proposal:** Six travel quests (flag + destination turn-in)

**Design:** Same pattern as Cattle Drive. Random start at location A, one-shot flag, finish on the location page at B as `> Quest: …`. No named ponygirl templates. Missing Mare keep-path uses `AddPonygirl` archetype (random name / race pool).

**How it plays**
1. Random event at the start town (or Wilderness for Bone Charm)
2. Inspect / Accept / Decline
3. Travel to the destination
4. Location tab shows `> Quest: <title>` while the flag is set
5. Turn-in pays and clears the flag (once)

---

**Quest 1 — Salt Run**
Harbor clerk needs a sealed salt crate inland.

- Start: Harbor Town (random)
- End: Goblin Village
- Reward: {MEDIUM_WAGE} gold + 1 repute

**Quest 2 — Iron Tithe**
Village elder sends iron bars to the post smiths.

- Start: Goblin Village (random)
- End: Trading Post
- Reward: {MEDIUM_WAGE} gold + 1 repute

**Quest 3 — Missing Mare**
A post merchant lost a runaway. Found behind the harbor net-sheds.

- Start: Trading Post (random)
- End: Harbor Town
- Return her: {GOOD_WAGE} gold + 2 repute
- Keep her: archetype mare (loyalty 0, arousal 40) and −5 repute
- Not yet: leave, flag stays

**Quest 4 — Harvest Cask**
Halfling farmer needs a sealed wine cask at the blue-awning inn.

- Start: Halfling Village (random)
- End: Harbor Town
- Reward: {MEDIUM_WAGE} gold + 1 repute

**Quest 5 — Tide Fish**
Harbor fishmonger needs tide-fish inland before it turns.

- Start: Harbor Town (random)
- End: Halfling Village
- Reward: {MEDIUM_WAGE} gold + 1 repute

**Quest 6 — Bone Charm**
Scout finds a carved cave-charm in the grass.

- Start: Wilderness (random, while traveling)
- End: Goblin Village shaman
- Reward: {MEDIUM_WAGE} gold + 1 repute + all ponygirls +5 loyalty (capped at 100)

---

**Routes**
- Harbor → Goblin Village (Salt Run)
- Goblin Village → Trading Post (Iron Tithe)
- Trading Post → Harbor (Missing Mare)
- Halfling Village → Harbor (Harvest Cask)
- Harbor → Halfling Village (Tide Fish)
- Wilderness → Goblin Village (Bone Charm)
- (existing) Halfling Village → Trading Post (Cattle Drive)
